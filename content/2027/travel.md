## Entering France

France is part of the Schengen area. Travellers from certain countries need a Schengen visa to enter France; see [France-Visas](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/), the official visa website of France, to find out whether you need one and how to apply.

## Getting to Grenoble

TQC 2027 will take place in Grenoble (France), the capital of the French Alps, nestled in the heart of a spectacular mountain landscape. Renowned for its vibrant scientific and technological ecosystem, Grenoble offers a unique setting where scientific innovation meets natural beauty.

While Grenoble's airport is mostly active in winter, Grenoble is very well connected to nearby airports:
- you can reach Grenoble from the airport of Lyon-Saint Exupéry using train or busses in around 1h,
- you also have direct high speed trains from Paris, count around 3h.

You can buy you train tickets from [SNCF connect](https://www.sncf-connect.com/) or [Trainline](https://www.thetrainline.com/).

If you want to reduce your carbon footprint by traveling by train across Europe, we also plan to setup a channel where participants can coordinate to take the same trains.

## Getting to Maison MINATEC

Once you are in Grenoble, you can easily travel using public transport, including trams. You can directly use your credit card as a ticket, just make sure to place it on a blue card reader before entering the tram. Maison MINATEC is next to the "Cité Internationale" tram stop. You can also walk there from the train station (15mn walk).

**IMPORTANT**: Google maps may propose you to go there via the CEA Leti. But this path needs a special access card. Instead, directly type the address `3 parvis Louis Néel - 38000 Grenoble` and you should be safe. For more details, see [this access map](https://www.maison-minatec.fr/acces/).
